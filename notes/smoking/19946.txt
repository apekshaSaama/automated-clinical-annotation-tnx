A 71-year-old gentleman presents to establish care with a new primary care physician after he had lost his prior physician due to a change in his insurance. He has a past medical history of erectile dysfunction, skin cancer, osteoarthritis, and a torn meniscus. He has no acute concerns for his visit besides presenting for an annual check-up. He denies any history of propensity to bleed, tobacco use, or lung diseases that would put him at risk of hypoxia, such as chronic obstructive pulmonary disease, emphysema, or bronchitis. He endorses daily unprotected sun exposure due to yard work and tennis with a prior history of skin cancer. On medication reconciliation, his only prescription medication was doxycycline 100 mg daily. He also takes saw palmetto, fish oil, multivitamin, and methylcellulose.
