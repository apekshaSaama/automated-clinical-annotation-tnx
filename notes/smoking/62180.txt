He initially thought that he had pulled his muscle in his chest. He described the chest pain as sharp, radiating to the back, and 10/10 in severity. The patient was cold, clammy, and short of breath. Prior to this incident, the patient had been healthy all his life without any medical problems. He never smoked, did not have hypertension, and had no family history of sudden cardiac death or any collagen vascular disease. There was no predisposing risk factor for aortic dissection other than heavy weightlifting.
